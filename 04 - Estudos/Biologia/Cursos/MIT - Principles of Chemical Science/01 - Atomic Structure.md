# $$\text{Introduction}$$
Many people have a common question in chemistry: how the electron and the nucleus were discovered at a very low-tech time?

In this note, we will delve into this question and we study the methods that were used to prove the structure of atom.
# $$\text{Late 1980's}$$
In the late $1890$s, the scientists around the world believed that the theoretical structure of the universe is "complete", because they had discovered the:

- Atomic theory of matter;
- Newtonian mechanics.

So, in $1894$, Albert Michelson - an north-american physicist who would later win the nobel prize - said: "our future discoveries must be looked for in the 6th place of decimals".
